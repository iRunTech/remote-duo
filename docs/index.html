Remote Duo Host

Install this on your computer, then control it from the Remote Duo iPhone app: trackpad, keyboard, media keys, and a live view of the screen.

Get Remote Duo for iPhoneFree on the App Store · made for iPhone Duo
Download for Mac

macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

Or install from Terminal

curl -fsSL https://iruntech.github.io/remote-duo/install.sh | sh
Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit · portable .zip

Or install from PowerShell

irm https://iruntech.github.io/remote-duo/install.ps1 | iex
curl -fsSL https://iruntech.github.io/remote-duo/install.sh | sh

x86_64 and ARM64. Installs to ~/.local/bin, no sudo needed.

Prefer a tarball? Download it from the latest release.

Then connect your iPhone

  1. Open Remote Duo Host. A book icon appears in the menu bar or system tray.
  2. In the Remote Duo iPhone app, tap + and pick your computer.
  3. Enter the PIN from the host's menu, or scan its pairing QR code with the Camera app.
