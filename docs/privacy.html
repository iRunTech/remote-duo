Remote Duo Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

In short: Remote Duo doesn't collect, store, or share any personal data. Your phone talks directly to your own computer. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no Remote Duo servers.

What the app does

The Remote Duo iPhone app controls a computer running Remote Duo Host. Mouse and keyboard input, your computer's screen, and its sound travel directly between your phone and your computer over your local network (or a private network you set up, such as Tailscale). That connection is encrypted, and it never passes through us.

Data stored on your devices

  • On your iPhone: the names and addresses of computers you've paired with, a pairing token for each, and your settings. These stay on the phone and are removed when you forget a computer or delete the app.
  • On your computer: Remote Duo Host keeps a list of paired phones (by device name) and its own security certificate in its settings folder.

Local network access

The app asks for local network access so it can find computers running Remote Duo Host on your Wi-Fi. It's used only for that.

Purchases

Remote Duo Pro and the free trial are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details and don't know who you are.

Third parties

On Windows and Linux, Remote Duo Host may download the OpenH264 video codec directly from Cisco (ciscobinary.openh264.org) the first time you use screen view. That download is a standard web request to Cisco's servers; no personal data is sent. You can turn it off from the host's menu.

Children

Remote Duo doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/iRunTech/remote-duo/issues.
